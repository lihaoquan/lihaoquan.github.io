June 2018

gAAAme

Game Concept

Inspired by PUBG and Fornite's competition, this card game is centered on game companies competing with each other to gain popularity amongst players.

There are 4 main card types. The Feature card, which is features that game companies (the players) can add to their game to gain player base (in the end player with the highest playerbase wins).

The Trap card, when drawn, will give negative effects to the player. The Action cards, which players can use to perform actions to compete with other players for their player base.

And lastly, the Player card, which represents the quantity of players each game company has, which comes in 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000.

Vandetta 1337: The RTS

Game Concept

1337 Vandetta is a single player Cyber Warfare Simulator. Players have to defend their systems and take down their opponents through various methods on the cyber medium.

Players control their character to navigate around a board that represents the internet, to set up their assets such as computers, servers, and database to generate revenue as well as use them as weapons to attack their opponent through the use of viruses and attacks.

In addition, players have to defend their existing assets through various defenses that they can choose to upgrade throughout the game.

NUSGDG Website Redesign

Title

NUSGDG - Website Redesign

Date

December 2023

Tools

  • Figma

Problem Statement

How might we clearly showcase the Game Development club’s activities and learning opportunities to attract and engage new members?

Project Description

I specialized in Game Development when I was in Polytechnic, which sparked my interest in joining the NUS Games Development Club when I first entered NUS.

I began researching, and one of the sources of information I used was the club’s website. However, the website was not very informative, and the entries on the page did not seem recent.

I knew that the club was for people who wanted to make games, but what events and workshops did they have? When, where, and how were they hosted? There were many unanswered questions and uncertainties, and I hesitated for a while before finally taking the leap to join the club.

This experience eventually motivated me to redesign the club’s website when I joined the exco team as a Web Developer. The goal was to smoothen the experience of information gathering for prospective members to encourage them to join the club.
