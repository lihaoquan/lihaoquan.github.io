Overview 概要

How might we help the RSAF train flight-line crew efficiently with Augmented Reality?

The Republic of Singapore Air Force (RSAF) seeks to enhance the training of its Flight Line Crew by providing a highly realistic and immersive learning experience. To achieve this, we propose leveraging mixed reality technology—specifically the Microsoft HoloLens—combined with game-based simulation. This approach offers trainees a high level of fidelity through kinaesthetic learning and functional realism, enabling more efficient, engaging, and effective training outcomes.

The outcome of this project is an AR application for the Microsoft HoloLens, and it was built in Unity Engine. This application was then further developed by two of my classmates who went on to join the Defence Science and Technology Agency as full-time developers.
